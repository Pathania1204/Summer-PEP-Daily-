Marvel's Spider-Man

Description

Marvel's Spider-Man is an action-adventure game developed by Insomniac Games and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. It was released for the PlayStation 4 in September 2018 and later for PlayStation 5 in November 2020. The game follows the story of Peter Parker, who has been Spider-Man for eight years and is now facing new challenges as he battles against various villains in New York City. The game features an open-world environment, allowing players to explore the city and engage in various activities while swinging through the streets.
